My World Papa, 💗

Ever since the 26th of July, 2022, my world has never been the same. That was the day you walked into my life — quietly, softly — and rearranged everything. The stars aligned in ways I never thought possible, and in the middle of all of it, there was you.

I have spent these years loving every version of you — your laughter that fills a room before you even say a word, your eyes that hold an entire universe, your kindness that makes everyone around you feel seen and cherished. Loving you is the easiest, most natural thing I have ever done.

You are my calm on restless nights, my joy on ordinary mornings, my home in every sense of the word. I love you on your radiant days when your smile lights up the sky, and I love you on your quiet days when you need the world to slow down just a little. I love you in every season, in every moment, in every breath.

I have searched the whole universe for words grand enough to hold what you mean to me. I tried the stars. The ocean. The dawn. None of them quite fit — because you are not like anything else that exists. You are entirely, perfectly, irreplaceably you.

And so, with every heartbeat that has counted since that beautiful day we began — with every beat that belongs completely to you — I ask you this, from the deepest place in my soul:

" Will you marry me, Papa? 💍 "
Will you let me love you for every lifetime that follows this one?

I promise to be your sunrise on darkest mornings and your calm in every storm. I promise to make you laugh every day, to hold you close when you need it, and to give you space when you need that too. I promise to grow alongside you, to cheer every dream you chase, and to be your home — always, unconditionally, forever.

Forever yours, with every piece of my heart 💗 Abinaya
